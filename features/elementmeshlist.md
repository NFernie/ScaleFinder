# Element Mesh List

Inventory of **Three.js element meshes** already built for the Wf type schematic, on branch `cursor/wf-schematic-panel-design-69e0`.

**Scenes:** `wf` (`src/core/wfSchematic.ts`, pin at 18.601312°N, 92.674937°W), `asym` (`src/core/asymWfSchematic.ts`, pin at Sfântu Gheorghe), and `tfw` (`src/core/tfwEcsSchematic.ts`, pin at −19.984295231442236, 119.165775381814, panel `TfwECSschematic.tsx`). All three render in [`src/ui/WfSchematicView.tsx`](../src/ui/WfSchematicView.tsx) via `geometryFor()`. Layout, ranks, nested poses, yaw, and explode come from core `sceneAt(explode)` — meshes do not choose where they sit.

**Axes:** +X alongshore (right flank), +Y up, +Z seaward. Land is −Z; the channel sits on the axis.

| Name | File | Geometry (size, shape) | Where used | Scene arrangement (position / taper / angles) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ground (water slab)** | Inline in `WfSchematicView.tsx` (`BoxGeometry`) | Box **8 × 0.05 × 6** (`GROUND_THICKNESS`). Blue, 30% opacity, `depthWrite` off. | `wf`, `asym`, and `tfw` — body `ecs`, kind `ground`. | Nested under origin stack: top of slab = water line (`waterlineY()`), below channel base. Parent of `ec-lobe` and `ec-mouth`. Explode: centre moves radially (`EXPLODE_SPREAD` = 5× at slider 1). |
| **Channel (half cylinder)** | [`src/ui/halfChannelGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/halfChannelGeometry.ts) | Custom `BufferGeometry`. Half-cylinder along **+Z**: radius **0.1** (`CHANNEL_RADIUS`), length **~2.57** (`CHANNEL_LENGTH` from `channelSpan()`), or a caller length. Flat face at **y = 0** (top); arc to **y = −radius**. End caps at **z = ±length/2**. Origin = centre of flat face. | `wf` and `asym` — body `e-channel`. **`tfw`** — body `e-channel-l` (longer run). | `e-channel`: nested in the mouth-bar complex at **(0, channelSpan().flatY, channelSpan().centerZ)**. **+Z** tip meets the thick end of the most seaward beach ridge; landward reach **1.55** beyond the landward ridge stack. No yaw. **`e-channel-l`:** parent `es-ridge-l`. Seaward end is one radius left of the convex set’s outermost tip. Landward end is the tidal channel’s landward left wall. Yaw follows that run. Flat face at the tidal channel’s Y. |
| **Tidal channel (funnel half cylinder)** | [`src/ui/tidalChannelGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/tidalChannelGeometry.ts) | Custom `BufferGeometry`. Flat top at **y = 0**, curved base. Faces point outward, so the orange material covers the flat top, the arc, and both end caps out to the mesh dimensions. The Tfw shell is **50%** transparent (opacity **0.5**, `depthWrite` off). Length **1.5× CHANNEL_LENGTH**, extra length landward; seaward tip stays with the half channel. Landward width **2 × CHANNEL_RADIUS**. Centreline stays at **x = 0**. The landward tip stays a semicircle (floor at **y = −CHANNEL_RADIUS**). The floor then dips in a straight line toward **+Z**. That dip angle is **1.2×** the angle from the landward floor down to the most landward mouth-bar base (`landwardMouthBarBaseY`), so the seaward tip is deeper. Constant width on the landward extension, then a **u²** flare that starts **1.2× CHANNEL_LENGTH** landward of the seaward tip (landward of the half channel's landward end), concave toward the centre, to **10×** width. | **`tfw` only** — body `e-channel`. Dispatch: `channelMesh('tfw')`. | Same nested pose as the half channel: **(0, channelSpan().flatY, channelSpan().centerZ)**. **Yaw +15°** (seaward end toward **+X**). |
| **Mouth bar (half spheroid)** | [`src/ui/mouthBarGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/mouthBarGeometry.ts) | Custom `BufferGeometry`. Half spheroid: diameters **X 0.5, Y 0.084, Z 1.08** (`MOUTH_BAR_SCALE`). Flat base follows sigmoid dip seaward (`MOUTH_BAR_DIP` **0.18**); dome on upper hemisphere (8 rings × 32 segments). Local **y = 0** on landward rim of base. | `wf`, `asym`, and `tfw` — **18** elements: `e-mouth-0…8` (seaward fan) and `e-mouth-prox-0…8` (proximal fan), kind `mouth-bar`. | Under `es-mouth` → `ec-mouth`. **Two fans:** (1) seaward V in front of channel; (2) proximal fan from landward ridge under channel, prograding seaward. Fan template **9** bars; X centres scaled by `MOUTH_FAN_X_SCALE` (doubled overlap vs original **0.32** gap → **~0.14** between inner bars). Each seaward step: **−5%** of bar thickness in Y (`MOUTH_BAR_STEP`), **+1°** yaw per step off-axis (`MOUTH_BAR_YAW_STEP`, sign from X). Landward apex crown aligned to **channel centre Y** (`mouthBarComplexLift()`). **`tfw`:** the mouth-bar complex is rotated about the channel origin so its landward-to-seaward centreline lies on the channel centreline. Yaws are that **+15°** axis plus each bar’s Wf fan offset, plus an outward side yaw: **0°** on the axis and proximal bars (template **|X| ≤ 0.16**), then **2°** at template **|X| 0.32** up to **5°** at the outer bars (**|X| 0.64**), toward **−X** on the left and **+X** on the right (`tfwMouthBarSideYawRad`). The seaward fan (`e-mouth-*`) is spread in channel-local **X** so its inner-pair overlap is half the Wf overlap (**0.18** instead of **0.36**, `tfwMouthBarXSpread`). The landward fan (`e-mouth-prox-*`) keeps the Wf overlap of **0.36**. Each bar's channel-local **X** is then scaled with the local half-width (`tfwMouthBarMarginScale`) so it stays the same fraction of the way from the centreline to the wall. |
| **Beach ridge (symmetric loft)** | [`src/ui/beachRidgeGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/beachRidgeGeometry.ts) | Custom lofted `BufferGeometry` from `beachRidgeStations` + `beachRidgeRing` in core. Alongshore **X 0.1 → 3.45** (channel → tip), **10** stations. Cross-section: crest + logistic seaward face (`RIDGE_FACE_SLOPE` **0.25**, base sheared seaward **0.12**). Half-width **0.13** tapering to tip **0.015**. Height thick at channel, tip above water. **Right** flank built in +X; **left** = mirror X + flipped winding. `flatShading` in view. | **`wf`:** all **8** ridges (`e-ridge-l-0…3`, `e-ridge-r-0…3`). **`asym`:** **right** flank only (`e-ridge-r-*`). | Per flank: set group at **x = ±1.7**, **4** elements indexed **0 = landward … 3 = seaward**. Nested Z step **0.34** at channel end; Y step **2%** of reference crest between ridges (`RIDGE_DOWNSTEP`). Plan centreline: landward bow + tip sigmoid; **20°** landward shear at channel on most seaward ridge, **12%** less per step landward (`beachRidgePlanAngleDeg`). Tips taper together; channel stays seaward apex. |
| **Beach ridge (asymmetric loft)** | [`src/ui/asymmetricBeachRidgeGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/asymmetricBeachRidgeGeometry.ts) + centreline [`src/core/asymmetricBeachRidgePlan.ts`](../src/core/asymmetricBeachRidgePlan.ts) | Same cross-section and taper as symmetric (`beachRidgeRing`). Plan centreline: **crescent** — seaward bow **0.72** above channel–tip chord, crest at **33%** of length from channel (`ASYM_RIDGE_CREST`), blends onto symmetric curve by **58%** length; **25** stations. Left flank mirror same as symmetric mesh. | **`asym`:** left flank (`e-ridge-l-*`). **`tfw`:** **right** flank only (`e-ridge-r-*`). Dispatch: `beachRidgeMesh`. | `asym` uses the symmetric left-flank poses. **`tfw` `e-ridge-r-*`:** Wf yaw and Wf **Z** stack. Each ridge's nested **X** (`tfwRidgeShift`) puts that channel-end centreline on the yawed funnel wall at the same world **Z**. |
| **Beach ridge (BRconvexSW)** | [`src/ui/brConvexSwgeometry.ts`](../src/ui/brConvexSwgeometry.ts) + centreline [`src/core/brConvexSwPlan.ts`](../src/core/brConvexSwPlan.ts) | Same cross-section family as the other ridges (`beachRidgeRing`), held **uniform from tip to tip** (channel-end half-width and height at every station). Plan: alongshore **X** is a semicircle; **Z** is half that radius and positive, so the bow is **convex toward +Z**. Both tips share the channel-end **Z**; the middle sits a quarter of the alongshore span seaward (`BR_CONVEX_SW_BOW`, about **0.84**). Alongshore reach matches the other ridges (**X 0.1 → 3.45**). **33** stations, sampled evenly along the arc. Left flank is the right mesh mirrored in X. Index does not change the plan. | **`tfw` left flank** (`e-ridge-l-*`). Dispatch: `beachRidgeMesh` returns `convex-sw`. Plan previews: `brConvexSwPlanSvg`, `brConvexSwStackPlanSvg`. | Same **Z** stack and yaw as the asymmetric right flank, on the opposite side of the tidal channel. Each channel end sits on the left wall (`tfwRidgeShift`). No extra yaw and no extra landward shift. |

## Core silhouette (no Three.js)

| Module | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [`src/core/wfSchematic.ts`](../src/core/wfSchematic.ts) | Symmetric scene: `beachRidgeStations`, `beachRidgeRing`, `channelSpan`, mouth-bar fan, `sceneAt`, anchors `WF_ANCHOR`. |
| [`src/core/asymWfSchematic.ts`](../src/core/asymWfSchematic.ts) | Copy of symmetric layout; left ridges use `asymmetricBeachRidgeStations` for centres/explode; anchor `ASYM_WF_ANCHOR`. |
| [`src/core/schematicScene.ts`](../src/core/schematicScene.ts) | `SchematicSceneId` (`wf` \| `asym` \| `tfw`), ridge mesh dispatch, and channel mesh dispatch. |
| [`src/core/tfwEcsSchematic.ts`](../src/core/tfwEcsSchematic.ts) | Tfw element complex set. Wf ranks. Mouth bars keep the two fans, swung onto the channel centreline. Yaws are **+15°** plus the fan offset plus up to **5°** outward. The seaward fan has half the Wf X overlap; the landward fan keeps overlap **0.36**. Each mouth bar then keeps its fraction of the local half-width. Tidal channel on the axis, seaward width **10×**, flare length **1.2×** `CHANNEL_LENGTH`. Right ridges use the asymmetric loft. Left ridges use `BRconvexSW` in that same Z stack, on the left wall. `e-channel-l` is a half channel on the −X side of the left set, extended landward to the tidal channel. Both flanks shift in X onto the funnel wall. Anchor `TFW_ECS_ANCHOR`. |
| [`src/core/tidalChannel.ts`](../src/core/tidalChannel.ts) | Tidal-channel length, flare start, and radius along Z. No Three.js. |
| [`src/core/brConvexSwPlan.ts`](../src/core/brConvexSwPlan.ts) | `BRconvexSW` centreline: half-height bow, convex seaward, uniform section tip to tip. Tfw left flank draws it on the left wall, in the right flank’s Z stack. |
