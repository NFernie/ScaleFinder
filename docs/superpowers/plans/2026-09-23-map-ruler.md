# Map ruler Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Measure a chain of map clicks, close it into a polygon, and copy that shape into the Polygon list only when the person chooses Add to list.

**Architecture:** Pure rules live in `src/core/measurement.ts`. The app holds one measurement or none. A floating menu outside the snapshot frame shows the figures. The white line and closed fill are a map layer inside the frame. Add to list appends a metres Polygon at the drawn centre and does not use the import alignment rule.

**Tech Stack:** Vite, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind, Vitest, Testing Library, MapLibre via react-map-gl.

## Global Constraints

- One measurement at a time. Measure does not replace an existing one.
- Done, while corners are still being added and there are at least two corners, keeps an open ruler. No area. Done is not offered after that.
- Double-click, while adding and with at least three corners, closes the shape and does not store a second copy of the last corner. The total includes the closing side. The menu adds the area.
- Done with fewer than two corners stays Adding and shows "Add at least two corners."
- Double-click with fewer than three corners stays Adding, does not delete, and shows "Add at least three corners to close a polygon."
- After Done has made a ruler, a double-click does not close that ruler and does not delete it.
- Delete clears Adding, Ruler, or Polygon and does not change the Polygon list. A second double-click deletes only a closed polygon.
- Add to list only for a closed shape. Name "Measured polygon", switched on, unit metres, next unused swatch, centre is the drawn centre.
- Segment labels are "Segment 1", "Segment 2", then "Total". A closed shape adds "Area". Area uses the sidebar formatters.
- Line is `#ffffff` at 2px over a 4px `#0f172a` casing. Closed fill is white at 0.2 opacity.
- The line is inside the snapshot frame. The floating menu is outside it.
- While adding, double-click does not zoom the map. Marker clicks and missed clicks do not add a corner.
- Import, switch, and the unit control do not change the measurement. A later unit change does not resize the metres Polygon.
- Collinear or zero-area rings are valid, show area 0, and can be added.
- The menu scrolls inside itself. Match existing 44px pressable controls, Inter, and the teal focus ring.
- Update `ScaleFinderPurpose.md` so measuring on the map is in scope. Saving a measurement stays out of scope.
- MapLibre behaviour is checked by hand. Unit tests do not mount the real map.
