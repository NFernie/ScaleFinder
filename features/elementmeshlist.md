# Element Mesh List

Inventory of **Three.js element meshes** for the Wf type schematic, and the four scales those meshes are built into.

**Scenes:** `wf` (`src/core/wfSchematic.ts`, pin at 18.601312°N, 92.674937°W), `asym` (`src/core/asymWfSchematic.ts`, pin at Sfântu Gheorghe), and `tfw` (`src/core/tfwEcsSchematic.ts`, pin at −19.984295231442236, 119.165775381814, panel `TfwECSschematic.tsx`). All three render in [`src/ui/WfSchematicView.tsx`](../src/ui/WfSchematicView.tsx) via `geometryFor()`. Layout, ranks, nested poses, yaw, and explode come from core `sceneAt(explode)` — meshes do not choose where they sit.

**Axes:** +X alongshore (right flank), +Y up, +Z seaward. Land is −Z; the channel sits on the axis.

## Taxonomy

Four scales, fine to coarse. Use these codes everywhere in this file. **E** is the only scale that creates a mesh. **ES** and **EC** are label groups (`kind: group`). **ECS** is the scene root and also the water-slab mesh.

| Code | Rank | What gets created |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E** | `element` | One drawable mesh (channel, mouth bar, beach ridge, or delta plain). |
| **ES** | `element-set` | One label over the **E** rows of one fan or one flank. No mesh. |
| **EC** | `element-complex` | One label over one or more **ES**, plus sometimes one **E** that has no set (the axis channel). No mesh. |
| **ECS** | `element-complex-set` | One per scene. Parent of every **EC**. Its own body is the ground mesh. |

A second mouth-bar complex (`ec-mouth-l`) copies the Wf offsets onto the half channel. A symmetric beach-ridge complex (`ec-ridge-hl`, `e-ridge-hl-*`) copies the Wf left-flank offsets onto the left of `e-channel-l`. A concave-seaward complex (`ec-ridge-cr`, `e-ridge-cr-*`, mesh `BRconcaveSW`) sits to the right of the asymmetric flank, copies that flank’s Y and yaw, overlaps it by **5%** of the flank’s alongshore span, and shifts each ridge landward so the channel-end centreline meets that flank’s outer tip. The `ecs` water slab grows (`groundSpan`) to cover that extension and still covers the original 8×6 area; `wf` and `asym` stay 8×6. `e-deltaplain` is a green opaque sheet whose top aligns with the crest of the most landward beach ridge, above the water slab, bounded by the landward toes of each ridge set and by the channels, out to the slab’s landward edge. The right flank shifts in X onto the funnel wall. Anchor `TFW_ECS_ANCHOR`. |
