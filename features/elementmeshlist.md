# Element Mesh List

Inventory of **Three.js element meshes** already built for the Wf type schematic, on branch `cursor/wf-schematic-panel-design-69e0`.

**Scenes:** `wf` (`src/core/wfSchematic.ts`, pin at 18.601312°N, 92.674937°W), `asym` (`src/core/asymWfSchematic.ts`, pin at Sfântu Gheorghe), and `tfw` (`src/core/tfwEcsSchematic.ts`, pin at −19.984295231442236, 119.165775381814, panel `TfwECSschematic.tsx`). All three render in [`src/ui/WfSchematicView.tsx`](../src/ui/WfSchematicView.tsx) via `geometryFor()`. Layout, ranks, nested poses, yaw, and explode come from core `sceneAt(explode)` — meshes do not choose where they sit.

**Axes:** +X alongshore (right flank), +Y up, +Z seaward. Land is −Z; the channel sits on the axis.

| Name | File | Geometry (size, shape) | Where used | Scene arrangement (position / taper / angles) |

| **Ground (water slab)** | Inline in `WfSchematicView.tsx` (`BoxGeometry`) | Box. **`wf` and `asym`:** **8 × 0.05 × 6** (`WATER_SLAB_WIDTH` × `GROUND_THICKNESS` × `WATER_SLAB_DEPTH`). **`tfw`:** same thickness; plan size is `groundSpan`, grown so the slab still covers that 8×6 rectangle and also the half-channel extension (both half channels, the left mouth bars, and the symmetric beach-ridge complex), plus a **0.4** margin. Blue, 30% opacity, `depthWrite` off. | `wf`, `asym`, and `tfw` — body `ecs`, kind `ground`. | Top of slab = water line (`waterlineY()`), below channel base. **`wf` / `asym`:** centre **(0, …, 0.4)**. **`tfw`:** centre shifts onto the union of that rectangle and the extension. Parent of `ec-lobe`, `ec-mouth-prox`, `ec-mouth`, `ec-mouth-sea`, `ec-mouth-l`, and `ec-ridge-hl`. Explode: centre moves radially (`EXPLODE_SPREAD` = 5× at slider 1). |
| **Channel (half cylinder)** | [`src/ui/halfChannelGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/halfChannelGeometry.ts) | Custom `BufferGeometry`. Half-cylinder along **+Z**: radius **0.1** (`CHANNEL_RADIUS`), length **~2.57** (`CHANNEL_LENGTH` from `channelSpan()`), or a caller length. Flat face at **y = 0** (top); arc to **y = −radius**. End caps at **z = ±length/2**. Origin = centre of flat face. | `wf` and `asym` — body `e-channel`. **`tfw`** — bodies `e-channel-l` and `e-channel-l-join`. | `e-channel`: nested in the mouth-bar complex at **(0, channelSpan().flatY, channelSpan().centerZ)**. **+Z** tip meets the thick end of the most seaward beach ridge; landward reach **1.55** beyond the landward ridge stack. No yaw. **`e-channel-l`:** parent `es-ridge-l`. Seaward end lines up with the most seaward `BRconvexSW` outer tip. Length is **2×** the tip-to-tip run; the extra length continues landward on that same heading. The centreline sits one radius outside the edge. **`e-channel-l-join`:** seaward end meets that landward end; landward end is the tidal channel’s landward centreline tip. Yaw is the heading between those ends. Both flat faces sit at the tidal channel’s Y. |
| **Tidal channel (funnel half cylinder)** | [`src/ui/tidalChannelGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/tidalChannelGeometry.ts) | Custom `BufferGeometry`. Flat top at **y = 0**, curved base. Faces point outward, so the orange material covers the flat top, the arc, and both end caps out to the mesh dimensions. The Tfw shell is **50%** transparent (opacity **0.5**, `depthWrite` off). Length **1.5× CHANNEL_LENGTH**, extra length landward; seaward tip stays with the half channel. Landward width **2 × CHANNEL_RADIUS**. From the landward tip to the flare start the centreline turns **45°** from **−Z** toward **−X** (the left beach ridges). The flare stays on **x = 0**. The landward tip stays a semicircle (floor at **y = −CHANNEL_RADIUS**). The floor then dips in a straight line toward **+Z**. That dip angle is **1.2×** the angle from the landward floor down to the most landward mouth-bar base (`landwardMouthBarBaseY`), so the seaward tip is deeper. Constant width on the turned landward reach, then a **u²** flare that starts **1.2× CHANNEL_LENGTH** landward of the seaward tip (landward of the half channel's landward end), concave toward the centre, to **10×** width. | **`tfw` only** — body `e-channel`. Dispatch: `channelMesh('tfw')`. | Same nested pose as the half channel: **(0, channelSpan().flatY, channelSpan().centerZ)**. **Yaw +15°** (seaward end toward **+X**). |
| **Mouth bar (half spheroid)** | [`src/ui/mouthBarGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/mouthBarGeometry.ts) | Custom `BufferGeometry`. Half spheroid: diameters **X 0.5, Y 0.084, Z 1.08** (`MOUTH_BAR_SCALE`). Flat base follows sigmoid dip seaward (`MOUTH_BAR_DIP` **0.18**); dome on upper hemisphere (8 rings × 32 segments). Local **y = 0** on landward rim of base. | `wf`, `asym`, and `tfw` — **18** elements per scene on the tidal axis in **`tfw`**, plus **9** more on the third seaward complex (`e-mouth-sea-0…8`). **`wf` / `asym`:** one element complex, two fans (`e-mouth-*`, `e-mouth-prox-*`). **`tfw` tidal channel:** three element complexes — `ec-mouth-prox` / `es-mouth-prox` / `e-mouth-prox-*`, `ec-mouth` / `es-mouth` / `e-mouth-*`, and `ec-mouth-sea` / `es-mouth-sea` / `e-mouth-sea-*` (the third fan copies the middle fan layout, stepped seaward with the same Y/Z deepening as proximal→middle). **`tfw` also** draws **18** on the left half channel (`e-mouth-l-*`). Kind `mouth-bar`. | **`wf` / `asym`:** `es-mouth` → `ec-mouth`. **`tfw` tidal:** three complexes under `ecs`; `e-channel` stays under `ec-mouth`. Left copy: `es-mouth-l` → `ec-mouth-l`. Fan template **9** bars; X centres scaled by `MOUTH_FAN_X_SCALE`. Each seaward step within a fan: **−5%** of bar thickness in Y (`MOUTH_BAR_STEP`), **+1°** Wf fan yaw per step off-axis (`MOUTH_BAR_YAW_STEP`). **`tfw`:** fans rotate about the channel so the centreline lies on the axis. Yaws are **+15°** plus **2×** each bar’s Wf fan yaw, plus outward side yaw **0°** on the axis, **4°** at template **|X| 0.32**, **10°** at **|X| 0.64** (`tfwMouthBarSideYawRad`). Middle fan (`e-mouth-*`) starts at half the Wf overlap, then the inner-pair centre gap shrinks **30%** (**~0.276** overlap, `tfwMouthBarXSpread`). Proximal keeps Wf overlap **0.36**. Each bar’s channel-local **X** scales with local half-width (`tfwMouthBarMarginScale`). |
| **Beach ridge (symmetric loft)** | [`src/ui/beachRidgeGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/beachRidgeGeometry.ts) | Custom lofted `BufferGeometry` from `beachRidgeStations` + `beachRidgeRing` in core. Alongshore **X 0.1 → 3.45** (channel → tip), **10** stations. Cross-section: crest + logistic seaward face (`RIDGE_FACE_SLOPE` **0.25**, base sheared seaward **0.12**). Half-width **0.13** tapering to tip **0.015**. Height thick at channel, tip above water. **Right** flank built in +X; **left** = mirror X + flipped winding. `flatShading` in view. | **`wf`:** all **10** ridges (`e-ridge-l-0…4`, `e-ridge-r-0…4`). **`asym`:** **right** flank only (`e-ridge-r-0…4`). **`tfw`:** `e-ridge-hl-0…4` on the left of `e-channel-l`. Dispatch: `beachRidgeMesh` returns `symmetric` for those ids. | Per flank: set group at **x = ±1.7**, **5** elements indexed **0 = landward … 4 = seaward**. Nested Z step **0.34** at channel end. The landward ridge stays at nested Y **0.04**. Each more seaward ridge drops in Y at the mouth-bar fan’s dy/dz (`mouthBarSeawardSlope` × **0.34**, about **0.0114**, `RIDGE_DOWNSTEP`), so index **4** is the deepest and most seaward and its crest still meets the channel top. The same count and Y step are on every beach-ridge complex in `wf`, `asym`, and `tfw`. Plan centreline: landward bow + tip sigmoid; **20°** landward shear at channel on most seaward ridge, **12%** less per step landward (`beachRidgePlanAngleDeg`). Tips taper together; channel stays seaward apex. **`tfw` `e-ridge-hl-*`:** element complex `ec-ridge-hl` and set `es-ridge-hl`. Each offset from `e-channel-l` is the Wf left flank’s offset from `e-channel`, rotated by the half channel’s yaw, so the mesh hangs to the half channel’s left the way the Wf left flank hangs to its channel’s left. |
| **Beach ridge (asymmetric loft)** | [`src/ui/asymmetricBeachRidgeGeometry.ts`](../src/ui/asymmetricBeachRidgeGeometry.ts) + centreline [`src/core/asymmetricBeachRidgePlan.ts`](../src/core/asymmetricBeachRidgePlan.ts) | Same cross-section and taper as symmetric (`beachRidgeRing`). Plan centreline: **crescent** — seaward bow **0.72** above channel–tip chord, crest at **33%** of length from channel (`ASYM_RIDGE_CREST`), blends onto symmetric curve by **58%** length; **25** stations. Left flank mirror same as symmetric mesh. | **`asym`:** left flank (`e-ridge-l-*`). **`tfw`:** **right** flank only (`e-ridge-r-*`). Dispatch: `beachRidgeMesh`. | `asym` uses the symmetric left-flank poses. **`tfw` `e-ridge-r-*`:** Wf yaw and Wf **Z** stack. Each ridge's nested **X** (`tfwRidgeShift`) puts that channel-end centreline on the yawed funnel wall at the same world **Z**. |
| **Beach ridge (BRconvexSW)** | [`src/ui/brConvexSwgeometry.ts`](../src/ui/brConvexSwgeometry.ts) + centreline [`src/core/brConvexSwPlan.ts`](../src/core/brConvexSwPlan.ts) | Same cross-section family as the other ridges (`beachRidgeRing`), held **uniform from tip to tip** (channel-end half-width and height at every station). Plan: alongshore **X** is a semicircle; **Z** is half that radius and positive, so the bow is **convex toward +Z**. Both tips share the channel-end **Z**; the middle sits a quarter of the alongshore span seaward (`BR_CONVEX_SW_BOW`, about **0.84**). Alongshore reach matches the other ridges (**X 0.1 → 3.45**). **33** stations, sampled evenly along the arc. Left flank is the right mesh mirrored in X. Index does not change the plan. | **`tfw` left flank** (`e-ridge-l-*`). Dispatch: `beachRidgeMesh` returns `convex-sw`. Plan previews: `brConvexSwPlanSvg`, `brConvexSwStackPlanSvg`. | Same yaw as the asymmetric right flank, on the opposite side of the tidal channel. The set moves landward by half one mesh’s fore-aft width (`tfwConvexSwMeshWidth`). Each channel end overlaps the left wall by **10%** of the local tidal-channel width. |
| **Delta plain** | [`src/ui/deltaplain.ts`](../src/ui/deltaplain.ts) from [`src/core/deltaplain.ts`](../src/core/deltaplain.ts) | Adaptive sheet, thickness **0.02**. Solid green (`0x15803d`), opaque, `depthWrite` on. The plan is rebuilt from the scene: top aligns with the crest of the most landward beach ridge; seaward edge follows that scene’s beach-ridge landward toes; channels are cut out; the landward edge is the water slab’s minimum **Z**. | **`tfw` only** — body `e-deltaplain`, kind `delta-plain`. | One element under `ecs`. In the current Tfw layout the height source is `e-ridge-hl-0`. Its crest is above the water line, so the sheet sits higher than the water slab. The seaward edge follows `e-ridge-hl-0`, `e-ridge-l-0`, and `e-ridge-r-0`. The tidal channel, `e-channel-l`, and `e-channel-l-join` are gaps. The sheet runs to the slab’s landward edge and across its full width. |

## Core silhouette (no Three.js)

| Module | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [`src/core/wfSchematic.ts`](../src/core/wfSchematic.ts) | Symmetric scene: `beachRidgeStations`, `beachRidgeRing`, `channelSpan`, mouth-bar fan, `sceneAt`, anchors `WF_ANCHOR`. |
| [`src/core/asymWfSchematic.ts`](../src/core/asymWfSchematic.ts) | Copy of symmetric layout; left ridges use `asymmetricBeachRidgeStations` for centres/explode; anchor `ASYM_WF_ANCHOR`. |
| [`src/core/schematicScene.ts`](../src/core/schematicScene.ts) | `SchematicSceneId` (`wf` \| `asym` \| `tfw`), ridge mesh dispatch, and channel mesh dispatch. |
| [`src/core/tfwEcsSchematic.ts`](../src/core/tfwEcsSchematic.ts) | Tfw element complex set. Wf ranks. The tidal channel has three mouth-bar element complexes (proximal, middle seaward, third seaward), swung onto the channel centreline. Yaws are **+15°** plus **2×** the Wf fan offset plus up to **10°** outward tapering to **0°** on the axis. The middle fan uses half the Wf overlap, then the inner-pair centre gap shrinks **30%** (~**0.276** overlap); proximal keeps overlap **0.36**. Each mouth bar then keeps its fraction of the local half-width. Tidal channel on the axis, seaward width **10×**, flare length **1.2×** `CHANNEL_LENGTH`. Right ridges use the asymmetric loft. The landward reach of the tidal channel turns **45°** toward **−X** up to the flare. Left ridges use `BRconvexSW`, shifted landward by half a mesh width, overlapping the left wall by **10%** of the local width. `e-channel-l` is twice that tip-to-tip length, extended landward, and `e-channel-l-join` runs from that end to the tidal channel’s landward tip. A second mouth-bar complex (`ec-mouth-l`) copies the Wf offsets onto the half channel. A symmetric beach-ridge complex (`ec-ridge-hl`, `e-ridge-hl-*`) copies the Wf left-flank offsets onto the left of `e-channel-l`. The `ecs` water slab grows (`groundSpan`) to cover that extension and still covers the original 8×6 area; `wf` and `asym` stay 8×6. `e-deltaplain` is a green opaque sheet whose top aligns with the crest of the most landward beach ridge, above the water slab, bounded by the landward toes of each ridge set and by the channels, out to the slab’s landward edge. The right flank shifts in X onto the funnel wall. Anchor `TFW_ECS_ANCHOR`. |
| [`src/core/tidalChannel.ts`](../src/core/tidalChannel.ts) | Tidal-channel length, flare start, radius, floor dip, and the 45° landward turn toward −X. No Three.js. |
| [`src/core/brConvexSwPlan.ts`](../src/core/brConvexSwPlan.ts) | `BRconvexSW` centreline: half-height bow, convex seaward, uniform section tip to tip. Tfw left flank draws it landward of the right flank’s Z stack, overlapping the tidal channel’s left wall. |
| [`src/core/deltaplain.ts`](../src/core/deltaplain.ts) | Delta-plain rules: top at the crest of the most landward beach ridge, seaward edge from each ridge set’s landward toe, channels removed, landward edge on the water slab. No Three.js. |
